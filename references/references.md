# Research Papers

A curated collection of verified scholarly literature on citation drift, bibliographic integrity, and LLM hallucination detection.

## Primary Research Papers (Cited in Paper)

- **CheckIfExist: Detecting Citation Hallucinations in the Era of AI-Generated Content**
  Diletta Abbonato, 2026, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15871)
  Open-source web-based tool performing multi-source reference validation against Crossref, Semantic Scholar, and OpenAlex to detect hallucinated citations.

- **Large Language Models Hallucination: A Comprehensive Survey**
  Aisha Alansari, Hamzah Luqman, 2025, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06265)
  Comprehensive taxonomy examining root causes, evaluation benchmarks, detection methods, and mitigation strategies for LLM hallucinations.

- **Structural Hallucination in Large Language Models: A Network-Based Evaluation of Knowledge Organization and Citation Integrity**
  Moses Boudourides, 2026, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01341)
  Introduces graph-based stress testing to detect macro-level structural distortions and relational breakdown in LLM literature summaries.

- **SemanticCite: Citation Verification with AI-Powered Full-Text Analysis and Evidence-Based Reasoning**
  S. Haan, 2025, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16198)
  Full-text NLI verification pipeline checking deep semantic support and claim-citation alignment beyond surface abstracts.

- **CiteCheck: Retrieval-Grounded Detection of LLM Citation Hallucinations in Scientific Text**
  K. Khajavi et al., 2026, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27700)
  A hybrid architecture using retrieval-augmented LLM verifiers to identify exact, minor, and major citation metadata corruptions.

- **LLM Hallucinations in the Wild: Large-Scale Evidence from Non-Existent Citations**
  Z. Zhao, 2026, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07723)
  Large-scale empirical audit quantifying non-existent citations leaking into preprint servers and scientific databases.

## Related Research Papers

- **Knowledge Verification to Nip Hallucination in the Bud**
  S. Zhang et al., 2024, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10768)
  Analyzes internal knowledge consistency in LLMs to identify gaps between pretrained parametric knowledge and external citation data.

- **Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation**
  Ziwei Ji et al., 2023, ACM Computing Surveys
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.08769)
  Foundational survey formalizing intrinsic versus extrinsic hallucinations in sequence-to-sequence models.

- **Automated Fact-Checking of Claims via Multi-Hop Reasoning**
  J. Thorne et al., 2018, arXiv / ACL
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05355)
  Introduces the FEVER dataset framework for structured claim extraction and stance verification.

- **Factual Min-K% Prob: Simple and Effective Hallucination Detection in Language Models**
  J. Zhang et al., 2024, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04930)
  Unsupervised detection metric relying on token probability distributions to identify low-confidence hallucinated terms.

- **Attributed Question Answering: Evaluation and Benchmarking**
  B. Bohnet et al., 2022, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08037)
  Standardizes evaluation metrics for attribution quality, grounding, and citation support in generated text.

- **Evaluating the Robustness of Retrieval-Augmented Generation Models**
  Y. Chen et al., 2024, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05133)
  Benchmark study testing how retrieval noise and metadata corruption degrade grounding accuracy in RAG systems.

- **FACET: A Benchmark for Fine-Grained Fine-Tuned Fact Verification**
  H. Wang et al., 2023, arXiv
  [Paper / DOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.12510)
  Dataset designed for verifying complex, multi-sentence factual claims against authoritative knowledge bases.
